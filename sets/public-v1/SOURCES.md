# Sources: public-v1

Clips trimmed from public datasets, Pexels stock footage and recorded test footage, for
local evaluation. **No clip is stored in or
distributed with this repository.** Licences as stated by each publisher; the underlying
footage of several datasets remains subject to third-party copyright, and non-commercial
terms mean these clips are for evaluation only, never for training a model that is sold.
